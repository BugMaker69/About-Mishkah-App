Privacy Policy & Terms of Use
Last Updated: October 26, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Mishkah, a comprehensive Islamic application designed to provide Quran, prayer times, Azkar, educational content, and personalized tools. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

We are committed to your privacy. Mishkah is a "local-first" application, meaning the majority of your data (notes, bookmarks, last read position, downloaded audio) remains on your device.

2. Information We Collect

Mishkah does not require you to create an account or provide personal identification (Name, Email, Phone) to use the core features.

Technical Data

We may collect limited technical information to improve performance:

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics on feature usage.
  • Location: Approximate location (processed locally where possible) for Prayer Times and Qibla.
  • Crash Reports: Logs to help fix bugs.

3. Local Storage & Offline Functionality

Mishkah is built to work extensively offline. The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Downloaded Quran audio files.
  • Downloaded Tafsir and Books.
  • Personal notes and User Created Content.
  • Favorites (Ayahs, Hadiths, Duas).
  • Reading progress (Last read, Wird tracking).
  • App settings (Theme, Font, Language).

Note: Some downloaded audio may be encrypted locally to protect copyright. Uninstalling the app will delete this data permanently.

4. Third-Party Services

We use trusted third-party services:

  • Firebase: For anonymous crash reporting (Crashlytics) and analytics.
  • Supabase & Shorebird: For backend infrastructure and updates.
  • Google Play Services: For location and map features.

5. Permissions Used

  • Internet: Streaming and downloading content.
  • Notifications: Adhan and prayer reminders.
  • Storage: Saving Quran audio and PDFs.
  • Location: Accurate Prayer Times and Qibla.
  • Audio: Background playback of Quran/Radio.

6. Data Security & Children's Privacy

We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit. The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
